# Grit and Growth Mindset

## Question 1: Paraphrase (summarize) the Grit video in a few (1 or 2) lines.

It is a speech about a teacher that believes that all we need in education is a motivational perspective. A characteristics that predicts success is grit, passion and perseverence. The best idea that she heard about building grit is called growth mindset, and the belief that ability to learn can change with effort.

## Question 2: Paraphrase (summarize) the  video in a few (1 or 2) lines.

Skills are not something someone is born with but it is something that can be built through practice, effort, and learning. Growth mindset means using challenges, mistakes, or feedback to improve. We need to focus on getting better, not just looking perfect.

## Question 3: What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?

- Internal Locus of Control means believing that your actions and effort can influence what happens in your life.
- Key point in this video is that you focus on things you can control instead of blaming luck, other people, or circumstances.

---

## Question 4: What are the key points mentioned by speaker to build growth mindset?

- Believe that you can improve.
- Accept challenges instead of avoiding them.
- Learn from your mistakes and failures.
- Take feedback positively and use it to improve.

## Question 5: What are your ideas to take action and build Growth Mindset?

- I will not blame others when I don’t understand something instead I will find a way to learn it.
- I will stay with a problem, make more effort, and keep trying until I understand it.
- I will treat errors, failures, and confusion as chances to learn and improve.
- I will focus on understanding concepts properly instead of just finishing the task.
